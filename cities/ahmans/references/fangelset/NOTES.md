# Hotell Fängelset / Kronohäktet

- The three-storey prison opened in 1871 and was designed by Vilhelm Theodor Ankarswärd. The hotel history page shows the original pale rendered cell block, narrow repeated windows, roof lantern and chimney stacks.
- The building still uses its OSM footprint (`w429898168`). The custom model adds the three-storey facade rhythm, hipped roof, roof lantern and chimneys while retaining the mapped outline.
- Visual reference: https://hotellfangelset.se/historia/ (viewed only; image not stored) and Kalmar läns museum record https://digitaltmuseum.se/021016588827/fangelset-i-vastervik-som-var-i-drift-1871-2007-numera-bedrivs-har-hotellverksamhe.
