# Landmark references

- **Västervik station** — “Stationshuset i Västervik.jpg,” photo by Vesper8590, 22 November 2011. Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stationshuset_i_V%C3%A4stervik.jpg. The photo was used as a visual reference; the model is a separate hand-built interpretation.
- **Sankt Petri kyrka** — “Sankt Petri kyrka, Västervik.jpg,” anonymous postcard, circa 1905. Wikimedia Commons, public domain in Sweden: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sankt_Petri_kyrka,_V%C3%A4stervik.jpg.
- **Hotell Fängelset / Kronohäktet** — the current hotel’s history page and its historic building photo were viewed for reference only; the image is not copied into this project: https://hotellfangelset.se/historia/. Kalmar läns museum’s photo record was also consulted: https://digitaltmuseum.se/021016588827/fangelset-i-vastervik-som-var-i-drift-1871-2007-numera-bedrivs-har-hotellverksamhe.
- **Ellen Key school** — *Ellen Keyskolan i Västervik: Bebyggelsehistorisk utredning*, Magdalena Jonsson, Kalmar läns museum, April 2021. Used for architectural facts about the 1870 main building and later additions; report images were viewed only and are not stored: https://www.vastervik.se/globalassets/bygga-bo-och-miljo/kommunens-planarbete/detaljplaner/sjukhusomradet-kv.-lakaren-m.fl/samrad/utredningar/bilaga-15-bebyggelsehistorisk-utredning---ellen-keyskolan-i-vastervik-kalmar-lans-museum-april-2021.pdf.

The station house was built in 1879 to Adolf Melander’s drawings: https://www.smalsparet.se/sv/?option=com_content&task=view&id=26&Itemid=46. Sankt Petri was built in 1903–1905 of red brick and sandstone in a Neo-Gothic Latin-cross plan: https://digitaltmuseum.se/021016588822/sankt-petri-kyrka-i-vastervik.

- **Tjustgatan 1** — Google Street View, August 2024, viewed only: https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=57.753353,16.644186&heading=126&pitch=8&fov=80. Used for the pale yellow timber siding, white trim, red tiled roof and entrance-porch details. No Street View image is stored.
- **Hagaholmsgatan 7** — Google Street View, August 2024, viewed only: https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=57.750854,16.650547&heading=206&pitch=8&fov=80. Used for the light vertical timber siding, white-framed windows and dark roof. No Street View image is stored.
- **V-DATA, Allén 4** — the official contact page confirms the address: https://v-dataivvik.se/kontakt/. Google Street View, August 2024, viewed only: https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=57.7572377,16.6290799&heading=140&pitch=8&fov=80. Used to identify the low, light-coloured gabled shop unit and its prominent red sign; no image is stored.
- **Corner, Allén 1** — the official website confirms the address: https://corner.nu/. Google Street View, August 2024, viewed only: https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=57.756641,16.629280&heading=343&pitch=8&fov=80. Used for the low pale storefront, dark roof, green awning and red Corner sign; no image is stored.
- **Sillgatan 9** — Google Maps/Street View, August 2024, viewed only: https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=57.7696115,16.6069979. Used for the white-and-dark half-timbered one-storey facade, low tiled roof and front dormer; no Street View image is stored.
- **Rådjursstigen 15** — Google Street View, August 2024, viewed only: https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=57.76771,16.610619. Used for the one-storey light timber house, red tiled gable roof, stone base and simple entrance steps; no Street View image is stored.
- **Västervik rådhus, Stora torget** — Kalmar läns museums photo archive, “Rådhuset i Västervik,” identifier KLMF.Västervik320hz, Public Domain Mark 1.0: https://digitaltmuseum.se/021016836600/radhuset-i-vastervik. A local copy of the openly marked public-domain photo is in `radhuset/radhuset-vastervik.jpg`. Google Street View, August 2024, viewed only: https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&viewpoint=57.7582568,16.6369978&heading=295&pitch=8&fov=80.
- **Ljungheden** — the mapped area is OpenStreetMap way 257022852: https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/257022852. Västervik Speedway lists its address as Ljungheden 1, 593 41 Västervik: https://vastervikspeedway.se/kontakta-oss/. The game label is placed at the centre of the mapped Ljungheden area; no image is stored.
- **Slottsholmen hotel and floating hotel** — Sandellsandberg's project description and photographs: https://www.sandellsandberg.se/content/slottsholmen-vastervik/. Used as a visual reference only; the images are not stored. The official hotel's history page confirms the 1913 and 1952 restaurants, the 2014 demolition and the current 2018 development with floating accommodation: https://slottsholmen.com/om-oss/.
- **Stegeholms slottsruin** — OpenStreetMap way 1126644152: https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1126644152. Västervik Municipality's pages on the ruin's lookout and ongoing conservation, and on archaeological finds: https://www.vastervik.se/Bygga-bo-och-miljo/Samhallsutveckling/Utvecklingsprojekt/slottsruinen/ and https://www.vastervik.se/Uppleva-och-gora/Kultur-sevardheter/kulturhistoria-kulturarv/slottsholmens-historia/arkeologiska-fynd-pa-slottsholmen/. The ruin is depicted as it stands today, not rebuilt as a complete castle.
- **Stegeholms borgruin photographs** — “Stegeholms borgruin, Västervik - 20260524 - 06.jpg” and “... - 07.jpg,” AleWi, 24 May 2026, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stegeholms_borgruin,_V%C3%A4stervik_-_20260524_-_06.jpg and https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stegeholms_borgruin,_V%C3%A4stervik_-_20260524_-_07.jpg.
