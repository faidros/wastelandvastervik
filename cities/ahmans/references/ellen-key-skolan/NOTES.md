# Ellen Key school

## 1870 main building

Kalmar County Museum's 2021 survey describes a three-storey building by Johan Fredrik Åbom, in a neo-Romanesque style with restrained neoclassical symmetry. It has a high granite base, rusticated facade, arched window surrounds, an arched frieze at the eaves and a pale yellow finish. The model uses the mapped OSM footprint and interprets the roof as a low green metal hip roof from the survey photographs.

## 1950s wing

The survey describes a functionalist school wing with a regular window rhythm, connected to the older building by a walkway. The wing is represented as pale rendered masonry with modern windows, a flat roof and a glazed stair bay.

## Scope and limits

The current OSM data tags the main building (`w312971950`) and the 1950s wing (`w391945967`) as school buildings. The 1936 addition is discussed in the survey but is not clearly separable in the available school-tagged footprints. The nearby generic building `w391945943` was left unchanged rather than risk assigning it to the school without confirmation.

Source: *Ellen Keyskolan i Västervik: Bebyggelsehistorisk utredning*, Magdalena Jonsson, Kalmar läns museum, April 2021. The report was used as a visual and historical reference; no report images are reproduced here.
