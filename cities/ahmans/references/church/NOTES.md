# Sankt Petri kyrka

- Built in 1903–1905 to Adolf Emil Melander's design, in red brick and sandstone. The church has a Neo-Gothic Latin-cross plan, a tall central spire, smaller pinnacles and pointed-arch windows.
- The custom model follows the existing mapped cross-shaped footprint `w391945935`, with intersecting nave and transept roofs, explicit pointed windows and a tall tower/spire.
