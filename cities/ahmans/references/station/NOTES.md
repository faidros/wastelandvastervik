# Västervik station

- The station house was built in 1879 to Adolf Melander's drawings. The reference photo shows a pale rendered three-storey frontage, regular tall sash windows, a dark hipped roof and dormers.
- The custom model uses the mapped transportation-building footprint `w1093394319`, with a central gable, dormers and a small station name sign.
