# Västervik rådhus

- Built in 1793–1795 to designs by Ture Wennberg, under the supervision of C. F. Adelcrantz.
- The building is brick with smooth plastered facades and a broken (mansard) roof, topped by an attic and lantern.
- The historic facade is symmetrical, with a central raised attic, clock, and lantern; the reference photo shows the date 1793 on the attic front.
- Street View from August 2024 shows a pale rendered facade, warm brown window frames and a dark stone base.
- The OSM footprint is `w1037123082`; its generic `building=government` tag is not a reliable storey description, so the model follows the historic photo and street view instead.
- Sources and image licence are recorded in `../SOURCES.md`. The reference photo is public domain (Public Domain Mark 1.0); no Google Street View image is stored.

The hand-built model is an interpretation of the available exterior references, not a measured survey.
